Guidelines

Do's & Don'ts

Do — Embrace
  • Obsidian (#050507) as primary background
  • Ghost (#ffffff) for all primary text
  • Emerald (#00d992) for brand accents and CTAs
  • Charcoal (#3d3a39) for all borders
  • Small border radius (4-8px) for precision
  • 4px-based compact spacing
  • Borders and glows over heavy shadows
Don't — Avoid
  • Bright saturated colors beyond emerald
  • Generic gray borders (#e5e5e5, #ccc)
  • Large decorative drop shadows
  • Dense text without breathing room
  • Serif or decorative fonts
  • Emerald for large background areas
  • Hard sharp corners on interactive elements
