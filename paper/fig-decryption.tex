\caption{Threshold decryption of one ciphertext of an application on pool key $P_j$. Every arrow into the contract is a transaction. The contract verifies a Groth16 proof for each partial and for the combine, and a Keccak Merkle path binding each partial's $D_{j,i}$ to the application's key; any committee member, contributor or not, may post a partial. The gate is the decryption window and, for an organizer-locked application, the reveal, which therefore comes first---the contract refuses every partial and combine until it---and happens once per application rather than per ciphertext; an automatic application has no organizer arrow at all (\S\ref{subsec:derivation}), and its combine proves knowledge of the secret $0$.}
